Our Services

Home Nervous System Reset

More than a clean, a complete nervous system reset for your home. Every space is meticulously refreshed with non-toxic products, while Reiki and intentional energy are infused throughout, leaving your home feeling lighter, calmer, balanced and beautifully renewed.

Bespoke Deep Clean

A deeply restorative spring clean, tailored 1:1 to your home's unique needs. Following a personal consultation, every space is meticulously cleansed from top to bottom with non-toxic products, then energetically reset with an infused Palo Santo ceremony, leaving your home purified, renewed and elevated.

Luxury Home Preparation

A bespoke deep clean for homes entering their next chapter, prepared for sale or ready to be lived in. Thoughtfully tailored 1:1, with organic, non-toxic cleaning, holistic care and plant-infused finishing touches, leaving each space immaculate, harmonious and beautifully considered.

One Off Luxury Wellness Reset

A beautifully curated one-off home reset for those moments when your space, and your energy, need a little extra care. Perfect for welcoming family, preparing for special guests, gifting a new mum postpartum, celebrating a milestone, or simply creating space to rest and reset. More than a clean, this is a high-end wellness experience combining meticulous attention to detail with a holistic sense of freshness, harmony and uplifting energy, leaving your home feeling lighter, brighter, beautifully restored and ready for what's next.

Luxury Construction & Builders Cleans

Exceptional spaces deserve exceptional care. With years of experience in high-end interiors, we specialise in the meticulous care of marble, quartz, natural stone, bespoke joinery and premium finishes. Our refined, low-tox approach and uncompromising attention to detail reveal the true beauty of every surface, leaving your property immaculate, elevated and ready to be lived in.

Commercial Cleaning

Elevate your workplace with a deeper standard of clean where luxury, detail and energy come together. Our bespoke commercial cleaning services are designed for offices, retail spaces and refined environments, creating spaces that feel immaculate, harmonious and intentionally cared for. Because when you elevate the energy of your environment, you elevate the experience of everyone who enters it: your clients, your team and your business.
